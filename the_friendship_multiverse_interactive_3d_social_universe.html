How to Explore the Multiverse

🖱️ Desktop & Keyboard Controls

Click and drag anywhere in the 3D sky to orbit. Scroll wheel to zoom in/out. Use A / D or ← / → to orbit, W / S to zoom, and keys 1 – 6 to fly directly to Islands A–F (0 returns to Overview).

📱 Mobile, Android & iOS Touch Controls

Swipe with 1 finger to orbit the archipelago, pinch with 2 fingers to zoom, tap any floating island badge to fly there, or use the bottom Island Dock and Orbit bar.

🎨 3D Asset Pipeline & Procedural GLTF Export
